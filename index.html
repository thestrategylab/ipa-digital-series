A monthly knowledge series by The Strategy Lab for the Indian Plumbing Association. Written for board members, chapter heads and business owners who direct the work rather than post it: why it matters now, how the pieces fit together, who should do what, and how to tell whether it is paying off.

Thirty minutes eachOne real market fact, one map, one topic, one fictional firm before and after, one thing to check this week.

No jargonPlain English throughout. Every number on a slide carries its source underneath it.

How to use this.

Presenting

Open a deck in Chrome, Safari or Edge and share the window on Zoom. Each slide is one idea; polls are run as Zoom polls from the question on screen, and the answer appears on the next click.

  • → SpaceNext reveal, then next slide
  • ←Back
  • FFull screen
  • NPresenter notes in a new window
  • PPrint view, then save as PDF
  • EscOverview of every slide

Sharing afterwards

Every deck stands on its own. Send members the link to a session, or the link to this page. The notes open on a phone and explain each slide in full, so nobody needs the recording to follow it.

The decks print cleanly: press P in a deck, then save as PDF for a chapter group.

Updating

The site is plain HTML in a public GitHub repository. Each session is one folder with the deck, the notes and the poll text. Every statistic is listed with its source in the data folder, so a number can be checked or refreshed in minutes.

Sharma Plumbing Works is a fictional business. Founded in 2006 by Rajesh Sharma in Kothrud, Pune, and now run with his son Aditya, it has 18 staff, excellent word of mouth and almost nothing online. Every session shows Sharma before and after on that month's layer, so no real member or competitor is ever used as the weak example.
