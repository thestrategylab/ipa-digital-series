# Session 1: Zoom polls

Paste each poll into Zoom before the session. Single choice. The slide shows the same question and options; where there is an answer, it is revealed on the next click.

## Poll 1: Guess the number (slide 2, minutes 1 to 3)

How many Indians were active internet users in 2024?

- A. About 300 million
- B. About 550 million
- C. About 886 million
- D. About 1,200 million

Answer: C. 886 million active internet users in 2024, 488 million of them in rural India and 397 million in urban India (IAMAI and Kantar, Internet in India 2024, ICUBE 2024).
