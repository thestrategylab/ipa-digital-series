IPA Digital Knowledge Series, Session 1

What digital actually means.

Not "being on Instagram". Five layers that hand a customer from one to the next, and most businesses only have one or two switched on.

Dhruv Tewari, The Strategy Lab, for the Indian Plumbing Association

Minute 0 to 1. Good evening, everyone. I am Dhruv Tewari from The Strategy Lab, and this is the first of six monthly sessions we are running with IPA. Thirty minutes each, one idea each, and I promise you will never hear me explain how to post a reel.

Today's idea is simple. When people say "digital", most of us picture Instagram. I want to replace that picture with a map of five layers, and by the end you will know which of the five your business has switched on, and which are costing you work. We start with a poll.

Digital is five layers, not one app.

Minutes 3 to 5. This is the map. We will come back to it every month, and each month we go deep on one layer. Today, all five, in one sentence each.

Home: where people land when they look you up. Your Google profile, your website, your WhatsApp Business number. Discovery: where people come across you and judge you. Google search, LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, reviews. Paid reach: paying to be seen by the right people. Relationship: turning one job into repeat work and referrals. Measurement: knowing what is working.

The point of the map is the arrows between the layers. A customer moves from one to the next, and a business leaks customers wherever a layer is missing. But first: none of this is new to you. Next slide.
