Internet subscriptions in India at the end of June 2025, up from 969.10 million three months earlier. Subscriptions, not people: one person can hold more than one.

TRAI, Indian Telecom Services Performance Indicator Report, April to June 2025, Press Release No. 86/2025. trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-09/PR_No.86of2025.pdf

Source is on the slide. Do not round it up to "a billion users".

Who does what.

Owner decides

  • Which layers matter this year
  • What success looks like, in jobs won
  • What the business claims to be and where it works

Team does

  • Photos from every site, same week
  • Replies to every review and enquiry within the hour
  • Keeps the customer list current

Agency does

  • Builds what the team cannot: site, ads, set-up
  • Sends five numbers a month, with sources
  • Never holds the logins

Three questions to ask them this month

  1. Which layers are we on today, and who owns each one?
  2. What did we post or send last month, and what did it get?
  3. What are we measuring, and where do I read it?

Minutes 19 to 21. Read the three columns briskly. The room is owners: they decide, they do not post. Click for the three questions and suggest they screenshot the slide.

One thing to check this week.

For your business

Search your business name, your trade and your city on Google, signed out, and look at what a stranger sees. Write down the first three things you would fix.

For your chapter

Do the same search for the chapter. Share the screenshot in the chapter group and ask one member to fix one thing before the next meeting.
