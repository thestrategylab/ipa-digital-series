### 2. Guess the number (minutes 0 to 3)

Use s02-01.

Poll: "Of 100 people who search on their phone for something nearby, how many visit a business within a day?" Options: 25, 50, 76, 90. Reveal: 76.

Source line under the number, exactly: Google, "How Mobile Search Connects Consumers to Stores", 2016 (US study of 1,000 smartphone users), thinkwithgoogle.com. Say on the slide or in notes: "US study, no Indian equivalent published; the pattern is the same here, and the next fact is Indian."

Why this one: the outline asked for either "share of clicks that go to the top three Maps results" or "how many who search for a local business visit or call within a day". The clicks figure (44%) could not be traced to a primary page (see `notes_unverified`), so the visit-within-a-day figure is used. It is a decade old; the slide must carry the year.

If Dhruv prefers a fresher number, s02-02 works as the poll instead: "How many times more likely is a customer to see your business as reputable if your Google profile is complete?" Options 1.2, 1.8, 2.7, 5. Reveal 2.7. Source: Google Business Profile Help, "Complete your Business Profile on Google", page read 2026 (Google gives no year).

Call made: s02-01 is the poll, s02-02 is the first fact on the topic slides. Note it in REVIEW.md under "Calls we made".

### 4. Idea 1: the Google profile is the home page (minutes 5 to 8)

Facts: s02-04 (9 billion connections a month, over 1 billion calls and 3 billion direction requests; Google, 2018, global) and s02-02 plus s02-03 (2.7 times reputable, 70% more likely to visit, 50% more likely to consider buying; Google help page).

Indian pair for the same slide or the next: s02-05 ('near me' searches in India grew 75% in a year; Google Year in Search India, 2018 data) and s02-06 (68 of 100 Indian SMBs completely offline; KPMG and Google, 2017).

Example: Neela Plumbing, Pune (ex02-01). Screenshot `s02-maps-neela-plumbing-listing.png`, 1440x900, shows: cover image with services (bathroom fitting, pipe fitting and replacement, water tank installation, drainage), name in English and Marathi, 4.9 stars, category Plumber, Directions/Save/Nearby/Send to phone/Share, full address in Mahalunge, open 24 hours, website, phone, Suggest an edit. The tall capture `s02-maps-neela-plumbing-listing-tall.png` also shows "From the owner" with a recent post, but its Photos strip came out grey (Google serves a limited view headless), so crop above the Photos heading if you use it. Review count could not be read headless; the firm's own site says 288+ Google reviews, and the presenter can show the live listing.

Interactive checklist (tick what your own profile has), from Google's own completeness page (s02-02 notes): category, services listed, photos and videos (logo, cover, work photos), business description, opening date, business hours including special hours, a review link you share, attributes, plus phone, website and service areas from the outline. Keep it to 8 to 10 ticks.
